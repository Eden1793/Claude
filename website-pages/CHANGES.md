# American English Pass — Ohel Website Pages

The pages were translated by a native Canadian speaker; this pass adjusts the text so it reads as natural American English. No content, structure, or formatting was changed — only word-level edits.

## Terminology (Canadian/British → American)
- **Physiotherapy → Physical Therapy** (main page, boys, Pa'amonim)
- **Speech and Language Therapy → Speech-Language Therapy**; "speech and language therapists" → "speech-language pathologists" (the standard US professional title)
- **children and young people → children and young adults** (matches usage already on the Families page)
- **post-secondary → postsecondary** (US spelling)
- **"open to the wider public" → "open to the general public"**
- **"entering the world of work" → "entering the workforce"** (Esti's story)

## Fluency and idiom
- "take an active part in community life / their communities" → "play an active role in…"
- "projects give real, measurable achievements" → "projects lead to real, measurable achievements"
- "go on to grow from strength to strength" → "go from strength to strength" (standard idiom)

# American English Pass — Oel Website Pages

The pages were translated by a native Canadian speaker; this pass adjusts the text so it reads as natural American English. No content, structure, or formatting was changed — only word-level edits.

## Terminology (Canadian/British → American)
- **Physiotherapy → Physical Therapy** (main page, boys, Pa'amonim)
- **Speech and Language Therapy → Speech-Language Therapy**; "speech and language therapists" → "speech-language pathologists" (the standard US professional title)
- **children and young people → children and young adults** (matches usage already on the Families page)
- **post-secondary → postsecondary** (US spelling)
- **"open to the wider public" → "open to the general public"**
- **"entering the world of work" → "entering the workforce"** (Esti's story)

## Fluency and idiom
- "take an active part in community life / their communities" → "play an active role in…"
- "projects give real, measurable achievements" → "projects lead to real, measurable achievements"
- "go on to grow from strength to strength" → "go from strength to strength" (standard idiom)

- Typo: "A WORLD OF POSSIBLITIY" → "A WORLD OF POSSIBILITY" (Sheffer Center section)
- "At Ohel education is rooted…" → "At Ohel, education is rooted…" (missing comma)
- Button: "Explore The Sheffer Center Network" → "Explore the Sheffer Center Network"

## Brand naming
- All 40 occurrences of "Ohel" / "OHEL" in the page copy were changed to **"Oel" / "OEL"** to match the domain name (oel.org.il). Filenames updated to match.

## US Chareidi audience adaptation (round 2)
The site targets Chareidi communities in the US, so terminology was adapted to frum-American conventions:
- **Hasidic → Chassidic**, **Chabad Hasidism → Chabad Chassidus** (Girls, Boys, Families pages)
- **Haredi → Chareidi** (main page, Boys page)
- **Lithuanian → Litvish** (Boys page)
- **Seminar Halichot → Seminary Halichot** (Girls page — "Seminar" is the Israeli term; "Seminary" is the US one)
- **"nationwide network" → "network across Israel"** and stat tile **"Nationwide Reach" → "Across Israel"** (main page — avoids reading as US-nationwide)
- **Phone numbers → international format**: 02-580-1616 → +972-2-580-1616, 02-580-2022 → +972-2-580-2022 (Get in Touch page)
- File renamed: `05_Oel_Paamonim_Bell_Center.docx` → `05_Oel_Paamonim_Center.docx` — the center's English name is "Pa'amonim Child Development Center"; "Bell" was a leftover literal translation.
